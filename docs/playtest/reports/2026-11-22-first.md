# Playtest: first (seed 12345, 512x512), 2026-11-22

I played 33 turns, from day 1 to day 20 03:00, opening the way a DF/RimWorld player normally would. Turn-by-turn notes are in `playtests/first/notes.md`.

## 1. Outcome

- **Day 20, 7 people.** 7 founders plus 5 settlers arrived, 0 were born and 0 died. **5 left, all "unhappy (no home)":** Wyna on day 6, Edric (the only Scholar) on day 10, Ilmen on day 14, Luna on day 18 and Morric on day 20.
- **Food: 303 meals = 14.4 days** (bread 76). 798 grain piled up uneaten. Farms: 88 wheat plots and 52 glowcap plots.
- **Built:**
  - carpentry: a saw horse, later replaced by a joinery bench
  - textile hall: a sewing table that made 16 straw pallets
  - kiln: a charcoal clamp, 130 charcoal at peak
  - underground kitchen at z214: millstone and oven
  - a surface kitchen with only an oven
  - 4 homes: one walled, roofed and floored, one open, one underground, one small
  - Stock at the end: planks 226, rough furniture 8.
- **Homes never worked.** Every home showed `beds 0, residents 0, fixtures 0` for the whole game. 16 straw pallets and 8 rough furniture were in stock, and on days 17-18 the whole colony stood idle. Mood stayed at 20-28% from day 2 on.
- **Industry:** planks, charcoal, flour, bread and straw pallets all ran. Nothing went past tier 1, and living standard stayed at 0%.

## 2. Timeline

- T1 (d1 00:00): stockpile, chop (25 trees), gather, carpentry with saw horse and crates, a home. 12 h later: 107 logs and a working saw.
- T2: textiles (straw pallets), kiln (charcoal), 88 wheat plots, a stair and a hall dug at z214 for stone.
- T2-T3: the kiln and textile blueprints sat unbuilt for a full day with 98 logs in stock. They were built only after `priority all construction 1`.
- T4: kitchen in the hall (millstone, oven, 3 input crates). Mood had already fallen from 50% to 22%.
- T7 (d4): I ran `cancel 160 200 320 340` to drop far-off gather orders. It also wiped the stockpile, all 88 sown farm plots and a kitchen crate blueprint. I re-zoned; the plants survived.
- T10 (d6): first departure (no home). Sabis, and later Halis, were STARVING with 180-210 meals in stock, 50-90 tiles from base.
- T15 (d9): `priority all industry 1` gave the first flour, after 300+ grain had sat for 4 days.
- T23 (d13): the underground oven had still made no bread. I built a second, surface kitchen with only an oven, and bread started within 12 h.
- T28 (d16): `priority all mining 1` finished a dig that had been open since day 10, in one day. Glowcaps sown at z214.
- T29-T33: everyone idle, hauling backlog cleared, homes still empty. I tried policy changes, walls with a gap, floors, a fresh home and rough furniture. Nothing was furnished.

## 3. Pacing

- **First day: too fast, then stalled.** 25 trees giving 107 logs and a working saw in 12 h is great. After that, nothing new was built for a day (construction priority). Mood also fell from 50% to 25%, and nothing in the report said why.
- **First week: a grind, and hard to read.** I waited 2 days on blueprints, 4 days on flour, 8 days on bread, 6 days on a dig, and the whole game on beds. Each wait ended, when it ended at all, because I guessed a priority, not because the report pointed to anything. Settlers came one at a time (d3, d9, d10, d13, d16, d19), never as a "wave".
- **After the first week: no progress possible.** By day 14 food took care of itself (glowcaps plus one working oven). With homes broken, people left about as fast as they arrived. The colony stayed at 7-8 people, and there was nothing to grow toward.

## 4. Friction

- **Why is my home empty?** The home line shows beds 0 / residents 0, but gives no reason such as "wants straw pallet", "unreachable" or "needs walls". I could not tell whether it needed walls, a door, floors, a policy or a stockpile.
- **There is no door order.** Yet the `walls` reply says "add doors with the Door tool". I made a doorway with `demolish` on one wall cell. That also removed the cell's floor and roof: "Demolished 1 wall pieces, 2 floors and roofs".
- **Default priorities hold a new colony back.** Construction, industry and mining each needed `priority all X 1` before anything happened. Nothing hinted at this. Idle or hauling colonists sat next to open work.
- **Crates are a black box.** There are no crate contents, no "waiting for flour" on a station, and no way to say which item an input crate takes. The kitchen that mixed a millstone and an oven with 3 crates never baked, and I don't know why.
- **No way to pause or cap a station.** The saw turned logs into 226 planks and the clamp made 130 charcoal, leaving construction short of logs. My only way to stop them was `demolish`.
- **`cancel` is all or nothing.** It removes stockpiles and farms in the box, but it does not remove homes. I could not un-paint a home at all.
- **Gather and chop areas send people very far.** Luna went to 183,305, level 276. The goods then came home one fiber or one log per trip, long after I had cancelled the order.
- **`view X Y LEVEL` costs a turn.** I spent a 1-hour turn just to look at the hall.
- People are often hungry while food is plentiful, because they eat only once they are back at base.

## 5. Bugs and oddities

1. **Homes never get furnished or get residents (T1-T33).**
   - Homes tried:
     - #2 (238,274..240,278): walls, thatch roof, plank floor, then one wall removed
     - #6 (230,270..233,273): open, plank floor
     - #7 (237,251..241,255, z214): underground
     - #9 (241,262..242,265): small
   - Conditions: dormitory and cottage policies, 16 straw_pallet and 8 rough_furniture in stock, colony idle with only 89 loose items.
   - Expected: beds hauled in within a day. This is why 5 people left.
2. **`policy X Y N` replies "No home there" on some homes.** Home #7: with `policy 237 251 0 z=214` and also without z=. Home #9: in the same turn I painted it (`home 241 262 242 265`, then `policy 241 262 0`). It worked for #2 and #6.
3. **The oven in underground kitchen #5 never baked** (237,258, z214). For 8 days it had 19-36 flour and 100+ charcoal in stock, and the millstone in the same room was working. The surface kitchen #8, with only an oven (236,270), baked within 12 h.
4. **Starving with full stores.** T10: Sabis was "STARVING ... at 269,311,207: going to eat berries" with 210 meals in stock. T12: Halis was STARVING at 237,265, "going to eat bread", with 185 meals in stock. Colonists hauling far from base seem to go from hungry to starving within about 12 h.
5. **Sleep looks far longer than "a fifth of the day".** For many turns (T5, T6, T19), 5-6 of 7-8 people were "asleep on the floor" at both 00:00 and 12:00. On T19 (d11 00:00-12:00) food stayed at exactly 91 meals for 12 h and no dig progressed.
6. **Misleading glowcaps message.** `farm glowcaps` on a home zone (237,251..241,255, z214) says "need a solid floor under a roof". The hall has both, so the real conflict is probably the home zone.
7. **Stale open-orders count.** "Dig 99" stayed for 7 days while the level view showed cells being dug.
8. **Sown plots survive cancel.** `cancel` over a box with sown plots removed the plots, but the plants kept growing and were harvested once I re-zoned. A good outcome, but surprising.

## 6. Balance

- **Wheat yield is very high.** 88 plots gave 352 grain by day 8 and 798 by day 20. One millstone (2 grain to 1 flour, 165 ticks) made only about 5-10 flour a day. Grain is not edible, so most of it is dead weight in the stockpiles.
- **Mood** fell from 50% to 25% for everyone on the first day, then stayed at 20-28% with no lever I could pull. Since colonists leave after 5 unhappy days, the first one left on day 6.
- **Settlers** came one person per wave (d3, d9, d10, d13, d16, d19), though the reference describes "waves".
- **Chopping is generous:** 25 trees in 12 h gave 107 logs. The 54 trees I marked far out on T10 mostly became a hauling backlog; loose goods peaked at 520.
- **Hauling** 1 log or 1 fiber per trip over 50+ tiles was the main drain on labour in the first week.
- **Charcoal:** one size-1 clamp outpaced all demand, even with two ovens (130 charcoal by d13).

## 7. Top suggestions

1. **Fix home furnishing and residents (bug 1).** Homes are the whole people loop; nothing else matters while everyone leaves.
2. **Give a reason on everything that is stuck:** home "needs X / unreachable", station "waiting for flour (crate empty)", blueprint "no builder (construction priority 4)", mood "-25 no home, -10 slept on floor".
3. **Use sensible default priorities** (construction, industry and mining at or above hauling), or show a notice: "open work, nobody assigned".
4. **Add station limits** ("make until 50 planks", or pause) so tier-1 machines don't eat all the logs.
5. **Add a `door` order** (the walls reply already mentions one), and make `demolish` on a wall leave floors and roofs alone.
6. **Add a filtered cancel** (`cancel gather|chop|dig|zones ...`) and a way to remove a home zone.
7. **Haul in bulk, or stockpile near the work,** so chopping and gathering don't create days of one-item trips. Let hungry people eat on the way when they are far from base.

## Follow-up (fixed after this playtest)

- Homes never furnished: haulers only furnished homes when no other hauling was left. Furnishing now comes first
  (commit "Homes get furnished in busy colonies"); on this colony every home got beds within a game day.
- Oven never baked: the kitchen made two products with one output crate (flour filled it). Rooms now report
  "needs 2 output crates (one per product: flour, bread)", in the game's Inspect window and the playtest report.
- Starving with full stores: colonists now drop work to eat at a sixth of a day of food left (was a tenth), enough to
  walk home from far work.
- The report says why things are stuck: room status, crate contents, what each home still wants and whether it is in
  stock, and how the colonists spent the turn. New orders: `door`/`window`, `erase`; `cancel` is documented.
- Not changed: equal-priority work goes to the nearest job (a big chop order outranks a far dig); the reference now
  explains priorities. Sleep was 33% only because everyone was homeless (floor sleep is slower); in beds it is 22%.
